## What the app does

The app records your store's product prices and, when a price reduction is shown, displays the prior price — the lowest price applied during the period of not less than 30 days before the reduction — that Article 6a of Directive (EU) 2019/2161 (the Omnibus Directive) requires merchants to announce. It also lets you import earlier price history under your own declaration, and export a per-product price-history record.

## Your responsibilities

- **The announcement duty is yours.** The app displays the prior price computed from the history it holds; whether your store's price announcements satisfy the law in each market where you sell remains your responsibility. The app does not certify or guarantee that any store is compliant with any law.
- **Imported history is recorded as provided.** History you import is stored as you supplied it under your declaration that it is true and complete. We do not verify imported history.
- **Honest activation.** The display for a variant activates only once a full 30-day window of history exists for it. Until then the app displays nothing rather than a figure the history cannot support.
