# Omnibus Prior Price — Privacy Policy

*Proofhouse Ventures Pte. Ltd. · Last updated: 22 November 2026*

Omnibus Prior Price is a Shopify app published by Proofhouse Ventures Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us"). This policy describes what data the app processes and why.

## What the app processes

- **Product and price data from your store.** When installed, the app records your products' prices and price changes (per variant and currency) so it can display the prior price described below. It does not read your orders, customers, or customer personal data.
- **Price history you import.** If you use the attested CSV import, the file you upload and the history rows in it are stored as you provided them, together with your declaration and the import's timestamp.
- **Store information.** Your shop domain, store currency, and app settings you choose (for example whether the display is enabled).
- **Shopify compliance webhooks.** Shopify may send us data-request and data-erasure notices. The app holds no customer data; on a shop data-erasure notice (shop/redact), all data we hold for that shop is deleted.

## What the app does with it

The recorded history is used for one purpose: to compute and display, on your product pages, the lowest price applied during the 30 days before a price reduction — the prior price that Article 6a of the EU Omnibus Directive requires merchants to announce — and to produce the per-product price-history record you can export. We do not sell, rent, or share your store's data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Storage and security

Data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure under the shop's own key space and is accessible only to the app's backend. Access to your store is via Shopify-issued expiring offline access tokens obtained through token exchange. When the app is uninstalled, its store access ends; the stored shop data is deleted when Shopify sends the shop/redact notice (within 48 hours of uninstall), or earlier on request.
